# openclaw
## Vision & Core Function
Personal AI assistant that runs on your own devices and connects to all your messaging surfaces (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, etc.). It provides a single control plane for your digital life, offering voice wake, talk mode, and a live canvas for agent-driven visual tasks.

## Technical Stack & Architecture
- **Language/Runtime:** Node.js (>=22), TypeScript
- **Communication:** WebSocket (Gateway control plane), HTTP/REST
- **Ecosystem:** pnpm-workspace monorepo, Docker, Tailscale
- **AI Integration:** Anthropic Claude (preferred), OpenAI, ElevenLabs (Voice)
- **Deployment:** macOS (native app), iOS/Android (nodes), Linux/WSL2
